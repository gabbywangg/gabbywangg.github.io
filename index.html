About Me

My name is Gabby Wang, and I am a 3rd year chemical engineering major and media arts and design minor. Ever since I was little, I loved creating things with my hands such as painting, crocheting, and jewelry making. I strive to work with the intersection of biological sciences and art. My inspiration comes from biology and the natural world, whether that be nature or deeper into the chemistry of biology. In nature there are endless forms and patterns to take inspiration from. As an artist, I am always looking for new ways to expand my knowledge of design and explore new techniques.
